# SheikhGo Nexus — Promotional Video Script Brief

You are a senior B2B SaaS marketing copywriter and video director specializing in fleet technology, GPS tracking, logistics, and AI-powered business software.

Voice-over is enabled in this chat. After writing the script, provide speakable narration suitable for both a professional male voice and a professional female voice.

## Goal

Create a complete promotional video script for **SheikhGo Nexus**, an intelligent fleet management platform.

The video should feel premium, credible, cinematic, and product-led. Avoid generic vehicle footage that does not demonstrate the actual software.

**Target audience:** Fleet owners, logistics companies, transport operators, vehicle rental businesses, and enterprise customers.

## Video Format

- Duration: Approximately 90–120 seconds.
- Scenes: Exactly 12 scenes, in the order specified below.
- Aspect ratio: 16:9 widescreen.
- Language: English.
- Tone: Professional, confident, natural, and enterprise-focused.
- Music: Modern cinematic corporate technology music, subtle beneath the narration and building towards the final brand reveal.
- Call to action: Drive viewers to Book a Demo at https://www.sheikhgo.com.
- Voice-over versions: Separate complete male and female narration scripts.
- AI visuals: Include AI-based animations and intelligent dashboard interactions throughout relevant scenes, particularly AI trip assignment, predictive maintenance, anomaly detection, fuel fraud detection, and SOS dispatch.

## Brand Emphasis

The brand name is **SheikhGo Nexus**. Use this exact name consistently in the opening, at least once in the middle, and in the closing.

Emphasize these capabilities:

1. **SOS Auto-Dispatch:** Demonstrate an emergency workflow using live vehicle location and the concept of connecting with the nearest available recovery team, subject to actual partner integrations and availability. Mention Pakistan and Dubai recovery partner coverage.
2. **Fuel Fraud Detection:** Show unusual fuel-consumption patterns and suspicious discrepancies being flagged for investigation. Do not claim guaranteed fraud prevention.
3. **AI-Powered Intelligence:** Show realistic AI-assisted fleet workflows rather than abstract futuristic effects alone.
4. **Product Credibility:** Use actual SheikhGo website and dashboard screenshots wherever possible. Use clearly labeled sample data when real data is unavailable.

Do not invent customer testimonials, performance statistics, awards, guaranteed response times, or unsupported functionality.

## The 12 Required Scenes

For EVERY scene, provide:
- Scene title and approximate duration.
- Visual and screenshot direction.
- Exact on-screen text overlays.
- Complete male voice-over narration.
- Complete female voice-over narration with the same meaning and natural delivery.
- Sound effects and transition notes where useful.

### Scene 1 — Smart Fleet Management Introduction

Create a cinematic opening with commercial vehicles, connected fleet visualization, and the official SheikhGo Nexus logo.

Introduce the platform as a smarter way to manage fleet operations.

Key message: Smarter Fleet Management.

### Scene 2 — Real-Time GPS Tracking

Show a live GPS dashboard with moving vehicle markers, route lines, online and offline status, ignition activity, GPS signal-loss alerts, and connectivity monitoring.

Demonstrate how operators gain visibility into vehicle movements and status.

### Scene 3 — Vehicle Health

Show vehicle health indicators, low-battery warnings, power-cut notifications, and relevant fuel or telemetry signals where supported.

Emphasize early visibility into vehicle conditions and operational alerts.

### Scene 4 — Driver Performance Scoring

Display driver scorecards and relevant driving, efficiency, punctuality, or safety-oriented metrics.

Do not invent real driver scores or imply that every metric is available unless supported by the product.

### Scene 5 — Predictive Maintenance

Show maintenance insights, vehicle-health trends, service reminders, and a dashboard identifying vehicles that may require attention.

Describe predictive maintenance as helping teams anticipate potential issues where sufficient data and integrations are available. Do not claim that AI can predict every vehicle failure.

Include tasteful AI-generated visualization connected to the actual dashboard workflow.

### Scene 6 — Shareable Tracking Links

Show an authorized user generating a shareable vehicle or trip tracking link and another stakeholder viewing trip progress on a browser or mobile device.

Emphasize convenient tracking visibility and appropriate access controls.

### Scene 7 — AI Trip Assignment

Show an AI-assisted dispatch interface that considers relevant trip requirements, vehicle availability, and driver availability.

Visualize a trip being assigned through an intelligent workflow.

Voice-over messaging should explain how AI-assisted assignment can help dispatch teams work more efficiently. Do not imply unsupported autonomous decision-making.

### Scene 8 — Electric Vehicle Support

Show an electric vehicle fleet dashboard featuring battery percentage, remaining driving range, charging station visibility, and range alerts.

Use live telemetry only when supported by the vehicle or connected hardware.

### Scene 9 — Document Tracker

Show the vehicle document management module with registration information, insurance expiry dates, fitness certificate expiry dates, and upcoming reminders.

Demonstrate a reminder appearing before a document expires.

Emphasize organized documentation and proactive compliance management.

### Scene 10 — Anomaly Detection

Show a vehicle route map and realistic alert cards for night driving, a sudden speed drop, and off-route or route-deviation activity.

Use AI-style highlighting to identify unusual events and direct the operator's attention to them.

Describe alerts as potential anomalies requiring appropriate review, rather than proof of wrongdoing or an emergency.

### Scene 11 — Fuel Module and Fuel Fraud Detection

Show fuel consumption, mileage analytics, relevant OBD telemetry, fuel entries, and unusual consumption patterns.

Demonstrate an anomaly alert that highlights a discrepancy for investigation.

Emphasize the value of fuel visibility, operational cost monitoring, and suspected fuel fraud detection.

Do not claim 100% fraud detection or automatic proof of fraud.

Reinforce the SheikhGo Nexus brand subtly through the dashboard interface.

### Scene 12 — SOS Auto-Dispatch and Final Call to Action

Create the most memorable scene in the video.

Show an SOS alert, the vehicle's live location, a recovery dispatch workflow, and a visual representation of connecting with the nearest available recovery partner.

Mention Pakistan and Dubai partner coverage. Do not invent response times, availability guarantees, or success rates.

Transition into the official SheikhGo Nexus logo and a clean website end card.

The final 8–10 seconds must prominently display:

**SHEIKHGO NEXUS**

Smarter Fleet Management. Greater Operational Visibility.

**BOOK A DEMO**

https://www.sheikhgo.com

Use the exact official website URL and make the Book a Demo call to action highly readable.
