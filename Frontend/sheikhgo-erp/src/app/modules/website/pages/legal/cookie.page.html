1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help websites remember your actions and preferences, keep sessions secure, and understand how the site is used.

2. How We Use Cookies

SheikhGo uses cookies to provide essential website functionality, improve reliability and security, remember preferences where allowed, and — only with your consent — understand usage and support marketing activities.

3. Types of Cookies We Use

3.1 Necessary Cookies

Required for the website to function properly. These support login/session continuity, security, load balancing, and basic navigation. Necessary cookies are always active and cannot be switched off in our preference center.

3.2 Analytics Cookies

Help us understand how visitors use SheikhGo (for example, which pages are visited and how often). These load only if you accept analytics cookies.

3.3 Functional Cookies

Remember choices such as language or UI preferences to improve your experience. These load only if you enable functional cookies.

3.4 Marketing Cookies

May be used to deliver relevant campaigns and measure advertising performance. These load only if you enable marketing cookies.

4. Third-Party Cookies & Services

Some cookies may be set by trusted third parties that help us operate maps, hosting, analytics, or communications. Those providers process information under their own privacy policies. Non-essential third-party tags are not loaded until you give consent.

5. Managing Your Consent

When you first visit SheikhGo, you can Accept All, Reject Non-Essential, or Manage Preferences. You can change your decision at any time using Cookie Settings.

6. Browser Controls

Most browsers let you block or delete cookies through their settings. If you block necessary cookies, parts of SheikhGo may not work correctly.

7. Retention

Cookie lifetimes vary by purpose. Session cookies expire when you close your browser. Persistent cookies remain for a defined period or until deleted. Your consent choice is stored locally so we remember your preferences on return visits.
