Sheikh Travel Group / SheikhGo ("SheikhGo", "we", "us", or "our") operates the SheikhGo Fleet mobile application, website, and related fleet management services.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, protect, and share information when you use SheikhGo Fleet. By using the application and related services, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy.

  1. 1

    Information We Collect

    Depending on how you use SheikhGo Fleet, we may collect account, fleet, location, and device information.

    Account and authentication: name, email, phone number, username, employee/driver ID, authentication tokens, organization details, and user roles.

    • Name and contact details
    • Email address
    • Phone number
    • Account credentials / tokens
    • Driver information
    • Vehicle information
    • Trip and operational data
    • GPS and location information
    • Device information
    • App usage / log data
    • Crash / diagnostic data
    • Organization / fleet details
  2. 2

    How We Use Your Information

    We may use collected information to:

    • Create and manage user accounts
    • Authenticate users
    • Provide fleet-management services
    • Provide GPS and location tracking
    • Manage drivers, vehicles, and trips
    • Send notifications and alerts
    • Provide customer and technical support
    • Maintain security and detect misuse
    • Troubleshoot and improve performance
    • Generate operational reports
    • Comply with legal obligations
    • Protect SheikhGo, customers, and users
  3. 3

    Location and GPS Information

    SheikhGo Fleet may collect precise location information when location functionality is enabled, including GPS coordinates, timestamps, movement, and route data.

    Location may be used to display vehicle positions, monitor trips, support safety, record history, and help administrators manage fleet operations. Location access may continue while a trip or tracking feature is active, including background operation where required.

  4. 4

    How We Share Information

    We do not sell your personal information.

    Information may be shared with fleet/organization administrators where your account is provided by an employer or operator, with trusted service providers (hosting, authentication, maps, notifications, monitoring), and where required by law.

  5. 5

    Data Security

    We use reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect information against unauthorized access, disclosure, loss, misuse, alteration, and destruction, including encrypted communications, authentication, role-based access, monitoring, and security updates. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

  6. 6

    Data Retention

    We retain information only as long as reasonably necessary to provide services, maintain operational records, meet legal/contractual obligations, resolve disputes, prevent fraud, and maintain security. When no longer required, we may delete or anonymize the information.

  7. 7

    Account and Data Deletion

    You may request deletion of your SheikhGo Fleet account and associated personal information via our account deletion page or by emailing {{ brand.privacyEmail }} with subject SheikhGo Fleet – Account Deletion Request.

    We will process valid requests within a reasonable period. Some information may be retained where required for legal, security, fraud-prevention, dispute, or accounting purposes.

  8. 8

    Children’s Privacy

    SheikhGo Fleet is intended for business and professional fleet use and is not directed toward children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children where prohibited by law.

  9. 9

    Third-Party Services & Cookies

    SheikhGo Fleet may use third-party services for maps, hosting, authentication, push notifications, analytics, crash reporting, and security. Those providers may process data under their own privacy policies.

    Our website may use cookies or similar technologies to maintain sessions, improve functionality and security, and remember preferences.
