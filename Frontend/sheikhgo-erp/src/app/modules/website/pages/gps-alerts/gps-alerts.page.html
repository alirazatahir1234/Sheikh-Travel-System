Road Speed Alert

Legal road limit — not one fixed number

Fixed Speed Limit alerts when a vehicle crosses a limit you set (for example 100 km/h everywhere). Road Speed Alert goes further: it checks the vehicle against the posted speed limit of the road it is on right now. City street, highway, or service road — the alert follows the actual limit for that stretch, with a buffer you can set so minor overspeeds do not flood your inbox.
