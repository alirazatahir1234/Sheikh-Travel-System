## Full description (Play)

SheikhGo Driver is the official mobile app for drivers on the SheikhGo fleet platform.

**What you can do**
• Sign in securely with your driver credentials  
• View and manage assigned trips through the full lifecycle  
• Navigate with live maps, ETA, and external map apps  
• Share live GPS with dispatch (including background tracking on active trips)  
• Check in / out for attendance  
• Submit fuel receipts with photo evidence  
• Complete vehicle inspections and manage compliance documents  
• Track earnings summaries  
• Receive operational push notifications  
• Trigger SOS in emergencies  

Designed for professional fleet operations — not a public consumer rideshare app.
