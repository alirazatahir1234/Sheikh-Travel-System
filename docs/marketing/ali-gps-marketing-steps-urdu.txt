Ali GPS Vehicle Tracking — Early Stage Marketing Steps (Pakistan)
Abhi earnings nahi — pehle customers aur proof. Har step par Cursor se help maang sakte ho (templates, scripts, checklists).

1. Clear offer likho: free trial kitne din, kya track hoga (live location, route, alerts), kya nahi.
2. Pricing simple rakho: per vehicle / per month — ek page pe 2–3 packages (Basic, Fleet, Custom).
3. WhatsApp Business profile set karo: business name, short bio, demo video, rate card link.
4. Facebook page banao: product photos, 1 demo reel, contact button WhatsApp pe.
5. LinkedIn pe short profile update: "GPS fleet tracking for Pakistan logistics" + city (Multan/Lahore).
6. Google Business Profile banao (Maps listing): address/phone, category Vehicle tracking / GPS, photos, hours.
7. Google Maps pe consistent NAP (name, phone, city) rakho — customers search karein to milo.
8. 60–90 second demo video record karo: map pe vehicle move, alert, mobile view — Urdu voice OK.
9. Local Facebook groups (Multan, Sialkot, Lahore transport/logistics) me demo post — spam mat karo, value do.
10. Post template: problem (theft/delay) → solution (live track) → free trial CTA + WhatsApp number.
11. Multan transport unions / goods union offices list banao — president/secretary naam + phone.
12. Sialkot warehouse / exporters / freight godowns ke owners ke contacts ikattha karo.
13. Lahore adda / truck stand / logistics offices ke 10–15 targets shortlist karo.
14. Har target ko free trial offer do: 1–2 vehicles, 7–14 din, no long contract pehle.
15. Union meeting ya office visit: 5-min pitch + phone pe live demo dikhao.
16. Truck stands / addas pe jao — drivers nahi, malik / contractor se milo, card + WhatsApp do.
17. Logistics companies pe cold call: "2 gaari free trial, aaj install?" — short script pehle likh lo.
18. WhatsApp outreach: personal, short — naam + city + free trial; bulk blast avoid.
19. Har lead ko CRM ya simple sheet me likho: naam, city, vehicles, last contact, next follow-up.
20. One-by-one follow-up: Day 1 message, Day 3 call, Day 7 visit/demo — mass blast nahi.
21. Trial start hone ke baad roz 1 check-in: map theek chal raha? koi sawal?
22. Trial end se pehle meeting: kya useful laga, kitni gaariyan, price clear karo, close karo.
23. Paid customer se 2–3 line testimonial + photo (permission se) — FB/WhatsApp status pe use.
24. Referral maango: "Ek dost ya warehouse bhejo — uski pehli month discount / free week."
25. Har week 5 new cold contacts + 10 old follow-ups — yeh minimum cadence rakho.
26. Jab stuck ho (pitch, price, Google listing, post caption) Cursor se usi step ka template maango.
27. Progress note: kaunsa step complete, kitni trials, kitne closes — short weekly review.
28. Pehle Multan focus, phir Sialkot, phir Lahore — ek city deep, phir next.
29. Fake revenue / fake logos mat dikhao — sirf real demo aur real customers.
30. Jab pehla paid customer mil jaye: usi se case study (Roman Urdu) banao, next leads pe use karo.

Yaad: har numbered step ke liye Cursor se script, checklist, ya message draft maang sakte ho — akela mat socho.
