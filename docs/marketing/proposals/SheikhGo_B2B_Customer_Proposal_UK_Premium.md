## 1. Positioning

One connected platform for vehicles, drivers, trips, and fleet visibility — GPS + fleet ERP + driver apps + Premium AI Assistant.

Designed for: transport & logistics, rental & leasing, staff/corporate transport, travel & transfer operators.

---

## 2. Phase 1 — Core GPS + Fleet

Live GPS (Traccar; Teltonika / Queclink / Jimi), vehicles & drivers, fuel, maintenance, trips/bookings/routes, driver mobile app, reports, access control & audit. Includes playback.

### GPS hardware — 4G recommended

| Class | Examples | Notes |
|-------|----------|--------|
| 2G / GT06-class | Jimi VG03, GT06 | Lower device cost |
| **4G / LTE** | Teltonika FMC920, FMC130, FMM130, FMC001 | **Higher cost** vs VG03/GT06; preferred for UK |

---

## 3. Phase 2 — Operations modules

Settlements, pricing rules, wallets, CRM/bookings, notifications, shareable tracking links, smart assignment, compliance tracker, customer portal (partial), rental, website CMS. Mobile-app CF/Blob migration **cancelled**.

---

## 4. Premium — AI Assistant & intelligence

**SheikhGo AI Assistant** (premium): in-ERP ops chat; not autonomous control; LLM may pass through. SmartOps, interval predictive maintenance (**not** ML), fatigue, driver score (partial), premium GPS alerts, WhatsApp (Meta setup), Fleet app. Roadmap not sold as live.

## 5. Technology & deployment

Angular Nexus · ASP.NET Core / SignalR · Flutter · SQL Server · Traccar. Cloud (UK/EU region preferred where agreed) / client cloud / on-premise. Typical: **3–5 weeks**. GDPR/DPA in SOW.
