The challenge

Fleet ops are fragmented

Today (typical)

  • WhatsApp / calls for dispatch
  • Spreadsheets for vehicles & drivers
  • No live GPS for the office
  • Paper fuel & inspection records
  • Hard to prove who changed what

With SheikhGo

  • One ERP for bookings, fleet, trips
  • Drivers execute on mobile
  • Live map + alerts for dispatch
  • Digital fuel, attendance, inspections
  • Roles, security policies, audit trail
